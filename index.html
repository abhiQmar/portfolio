🔒 Architecture: How Your Private Key Stays 100% Safe

Why this is completely safe on public GitHub Pages:
  • NO Private Keys or Secrets in Git: Uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 User Consent (PKCE). Only a public Client ID is used.
  • Google Authenticates Your Identity: When you click "Sign in with Google", you authenticate directly on Google's login server.
  • Strict Sheet Access Control: Only your authorized Google account has view rights on the spreadsheet. Any unauthorized visitor on the internet gets 403 Forbidden from Google!
